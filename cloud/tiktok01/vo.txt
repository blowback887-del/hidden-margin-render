This mattress store looks empty every time you drive by. So why won't it close?
Reddit says money laundering.
Planet Money looked. No evidence.

The real answer is more boring. And more annoying.
Store owners told one professor they need twelve to twenty mattress sales a month to cover costs.
That's less than one a day. Every sale after that is gravy.
On a thousand dollar mattress, one analyst says the store keeps about six hundred before rent.
The biggest chain, Mattress Firm, bought so many rivals it had stores across the street from each other.
In twenty eighteen it went bankrupt and closed over six hundred. Still here, though. Twenty-one hundred stores.
So here's the move. Never pay the sticker price.
Consumer Reports says prices can drop fifty percent in a sale. In their survey, only twenty-two percent of shoppers even tried to haggle.
That empty store isn't waiting for a crowd. It's waiting for you. To not haggle.
