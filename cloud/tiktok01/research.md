## Facts (each with source)
1. Reddit theory: a Reddit user wrote Mattress Firm "is some sort of giant money-laundering scheme"; Planet Money found no evidence of money laundering. — NPR Planet Money transcript https://www.npr.org/transcripts/676543180
2. On weekdays a store "might only sell a couple of mattresses" (Michael Magnuson, GoodBed). — same NPR transcript
3. Store owners told Rice professor Utpal Dholakia they need to sell "a dozen to 20 mattresses a month to essentially cover their costs." — Freakonomics https://freakonomics.com/podcast/are-we-in-a-mattress-store-bubble/
4. On a $1,000 mattress, "a mattress store would be walking away with $600" at gross level (Brad Thomas, KeyBanc analyst). — Freakonomics, same URL
5. Markup "40, 50 percent, 100 percent, and even more" (Dholakia). — Freakonomics, same URL
6. Mattress Firm bought competitors (Sleepy's, Sleep Train, Mattress Giant) and "in many cases, they'd end up with stores across the street from each other." Grew from 700 to 3,300 stores in five years; filed for bankruptcy October 2018; closed more than 600 stores. — NPR transcript
7. Mattress Firm today: 2,100+ owned stores across 46 U.S. states, part of Somnigroup (parent of Tempur Sealy: Tempur-Pedic, Sealy, Stearns & Foster). Acquisition completed Feb 5, 2025. — Somnigroup Investor Day deck, Mar 2026 https://s204.q4cdn.com/436357164/files/doc_presentations/2026/Mar/04/Website-Version-SGI-2026-Investor-Day-Consolidated-Deck-03-03-2026.pdf ; 10-K 2024 via https://www.stratrix.com/companies/somnigroup-international/acquisitions
8. Consumer Reports: markups are huge — retailers can lower prices by 50% or more during frequent sales; negotiate at specialty chains. — https://www.consumerreports.org/mattresses/the-right-mattress-at-the-right-price/
9. Only 22% of 6,000+ CR members surveyed tried negotiating on their recent mattress purchase; "Mattress customers should haggle because it works" (Martin Lachter, CR, 2023 survey). — https://www.consumerreports.org/home-garden/mattresses/haggling-for-a-mattress-could-help-you-save-a2352446856

## Author's calc
