# T09 — материалы для загрузки

## Название
Why the Outlet Sells a $499 Bag for $169

## Описание
That outlet tag says "comparable value $499." You pay $169. You feel like you won. But according to a 2026 lawsuit, $499 was rarely, if ever, a real price at that store.

We take one outlet price tag apart: who writes the big number, why most outlet clothes are made for the outlet, the labels that give it away, and the CEO who tried to get rid of fake prices (and what happened to him).

3 things to do next time:
1. Cover the big number. Would you pay the small one if it stood alone?
2. Check the label inside (diamonds, dots, "1818").
3. Take ten seconds with your phone.

All lawsuits mentioned are allegations; the companies deny wrongdoing or the cases are unresolved.
