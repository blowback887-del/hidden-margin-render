## Instacart: Same Cart, Different Price (Groundwork Collaborative, Consumer Reports, More Perfect Union, декабрь 2025)
URL: https://www.consumerreports.org/media-room/press-releases/2025/12/new-report-exposes-instacarts-hidden-price-games/
- 400+ shoppers (CBS/AOL: 437 volunteers), 4 cities: North Canton OH, Saint Paul MN, Seattle WA, Washington DC; Safeway and Target.
- Experiments found at Albertsons, Costco, Kroger, Safeway, Sprouts, Target. Enabled by Eversight (AI pricing software).
- Nearly three-quarters of items shown at different prices; up to five prices at the same time.
- Dozen Lucerne eggs, Safeway Washington DC: $3.99, $4.28, $4.59, $4.69, $4.79.
- Basics avg gap 13%, highest up to 23% above lowest. Wheat Thins example — 23% (Lindsay Owens).
- Basket totals varied ~7% on average.
- Some sale items: sale price same, "original" price changed — discount looks bigger.
- Est. ~$1,200/yr for household of four.
- Instacart: acknowledges price tests; end shoppers not aware they're in an experiment; calls experiments randomized and "negligible".
- Owens: "pricing roulette". Brookman (CR): hidden algorithmic pricing without telling customers.
- Seattle Safeway, Sept 4 2025: 39 volunteers, 20 identical products, 5 different basket totals $114.34–$123.93 ($9.59, 8.4%). https://www.consumerreports.org/money/questionable-business-practices/instacart-ai-pricing-experiment-inflating-grocery-bills-a1142182490/ ; https://www.aol.com/articles/same-groceries-same-store-why-163709697.html
- CR survey Sept 2025: 72% of Instacart users didn't want different prices for different users.
- Dec 22 2025 update: Instacart stopped offering the technology that allowed retailers to charge different prices for same groceries at same time (CR article update).
- "smart rounding" — internal email term (CR; TechSpot https://www.techspot.com/news/110569-instacart-algorithm-quietly-charging-people-different-prices-same.html)
