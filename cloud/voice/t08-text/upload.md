## Название
McDonald's Said $9. The Menu Said $18.

## Описание
A Big Mac Meal for $17.59. McDonald's says the average is $9.29. Both can be true, and the reason is the real story: how the cheap meal quietly left the menu board in 2019, prices went up about 40%, and then the "discount" came back, measured from the new prices.

How to keep your money:
• The board is the opening bid. Before you order, check three numbers: the combo on the board, the three items added up, and the deal in the app. Take the lowest.
• If there's a value meal, ask for it by name.
• Prices are set by each franchise owner. The McDonald's at the next exit may be cheaper.

Our math (≈ +19%) is an illustration using McDonald's own average prices (2019 vs 2024) and the advertised 15% combo savings. It is not the price at any specific restaurant. Prices vary by location.
