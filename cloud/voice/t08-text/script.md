# T08 — McDonald's Said $9. The Menu Said $18. — сценарий (версия 2, после фактчекера)
Окраска: детектив («куда делся дешёвый обед и кто его вернул за наш же счёт») + сухая сатира на корпоративный язык. Злодей — не кассир и не франчайзи, а схема «подними — убери скидку — продай скидку обратно».
Сквозная линия: один Big Mac Meal — от фото меню за $17.59 до «Extra Value Meal» 2025 года. Предмет-талисман: табло меню (menu board) — Кемпчински сам назвал его главным.
Одна фраза: McDonald's тихо убрала скидку на комбо, подняла меню на 40% и продала скидку обратно, посчитав её от уже поднятых цен.
Под ТВ и зрителя 60+: обе суммы — вслух, цифра на экране держится ≥3 с, одна цифра на кадр.
Строки `> SEE:` — что видим (не озвучиваются).

## PART 1

Seventeen dollars and fifty-nine cents. For a Big Mac, fries and a soft drink. [PAUSE]
> SEE: фото табло McDonald's (скриншот поста X, ник скрыт), строка «Big Mac Meal $17.59» — крупный наезд, цифра держится

That's a real menu board, at a McDonald's on a highway rest stop in Connecticut. Somebody took a picture in twenty twenty-three, and the internet lost its mind.
> SEE: лента X: пост, ответы, ретвиты; плашка «Darien, CT · 2023»

McDonald's didn't ignore it. The president of McDonald's USA wrote an open letter to, quote, "our U.S. fans." And in that letter and its fact sheet, McDonald's gave its own number. In twenty twenty-four, the average Big Mac Meal in America cost nine dollars and twenty-nine cents.
> SEE: скриншот письма Joe Erlinger на corporate.mcdonalds.com; выделено «our U.S. fans»; затем PDF «Myths vs. Facts» — строка «Big Mac Meal … $9.29»

So which one is it? Nine, or eighteen? [PAUSE]
> SEE: два чека рядом: «$9.29 — McDonald's» и «$17.59 — the menu»

It can be both. Because the price on that board isn't set by McDonald's. More than ninety-five percent of McDonald's in America belong to franchise owners, and each owner sets the prices on his own board.
> SEE: карта США, точки ресторанов; на одной точке — табло с ценой; плашка «95% franchised · owners set prices»

Which means the McDonald's five minutes down the road can charge you less for the exact same tray. Same burger. Same fries. Different board.
> SEE: две вывески McDonald's на одной улице (сток), над каждой — свой ценник на один и тот же поднос

And look at where the eighteen-dollar one was. A highway rest stop. You're tired, the kids are hungry, and you're not about to leave the highway. Not many people there are shopping around. It looks a lot like the price was set for a customer who has no other choice.
> SEE: трасса ночью, указатель «Service Area»; семья выходит из машины; плашка «no other choice»

That's the first thing you can use today. But the real story is bigger than one rest stop. Because the letter also showed how fast the average price climbed, and what McDonald's did next is the part that should bother you.
> SEE: письмо снова; камера наезжает на таблицу цифр

## PART 2

Let's read their own numbers. In twenty nineteen, the average Big Mac Meal cost seven twenty-nine. By twenty twenty-four, nine twenty-nine. Two dollars more for the same tray.
> SEE: табло-счётчик: «$7.29 → $9.29», цифры щёлкают как на старом табло

McDonald's put that in a chart called "Myths versus Facts," to show it wasn't as bad as the internet said. Fair enough. The internet said a hundred and four percent. McDonald's says the real number was twenty-seven.
> SEE: PDF таблицы, строка «MYTH: +104%» перечёркнута, рядом «FACT: +27%»

But look further down the same chart. Medium fries. Two twenty-nine in twenty nineteen. Three twenty-nine five years later. That's forty-four percent. [PAUSE]
> SEE: крупно картошка фри; ценник «$2.29» → «$3.29», плашка «+44%»

Potatoes, oil and salt. The cheapest thing on the tray got the biggest percentage raise on McDonald's own list.
> SEE: сырая картошка на доске, рядом золотой ценник «+44%» — сухая шутка

And across the whole menu, by the company's own fact sheet, the average item went up forty percent in five years.
> SEE: всё табло меню целиком, по нему прокатывается волна «+40%»

McDonald's says that just kept up with costs. Beef, wages, paper. And costs really did go up. But here's another number. Between twenty nineteen and twenty twenty-three, McDonald's gross profit grew from about eleven billion dollars a year to more than fourteen and a half. Up about thirty percent. [PAUSE]
> SEE: заголовок Reuters (через Yahoo) про прибыль; столбики «$11.18B (2019)» → «$14.56B (2023)», плашка «+30%»

Costs went up for everybody. Only one side ended up with more.
> SEE: весы: на одной чаше «your bill», на другой «their profit»; чаша прибыли перевешивает

So if the average meal went up "only" twenty-seven percent, why did so many people feel like they got robbed? [PAUSE]
> SEE: человек в машине у окна раздачи смотрит на чек (сток)

The answer isn't in the price of the burger. It's in something McDonald's took off the board.
> SEE: табло, на котором пустеет одна строка

## PART 3

Remember the Extra Value Meal? Pick a number, get a sandwich, fries and a drink, and pay less than buying them one by one. That was the whole deal. The meal was the discount.
> SEE: архивное фото/реклама меню с «Extra Value Meal» (90-е — 2000-е, с правами); плашка «the meal = the discount»

In twenty nineteen, McDonald's quietly dropped Extra Value Meals as a menu category. No funeral. No announcement you'd remember. It just faded off the board.
> SEE: старое табло; строка «Extra Value Meals» растворяется; дата «2019»

Then came the pandemic, then inflation, and prices on the board kept climbing. By last year, the CEO, Chris Kempczinski, said it out loud on a call with investors. Customers were, quote, "too often" seeing combo meals that cost more than ten dollars.
> SEE: заголовок CNN/KSL с цитатой «too often… more than $10»; цитата крупно поверх стока зала McDonald's

He also said the single biggest thing that shapes what people think of McDonald's value… is the menu board. [PAUSE]
> SEE: цитата «The single biggest driver… is the menu board.» — табло McDonald's в кадре

The menu board. The thing franchise owners had been raising for five years.
> SEE: табло, цены по одной прибавляют по доллару

And the people who noticed first were the people who could least afford it. In late twenty twenty-five, Kempczinski told investors that across fast food, visits from lower-income customers had been falling by nearly double digits. For almost two years. Visits from higher earners were growing about as fast.
> SEE: график-«ножницы»: линия low-income вниз, high-income вверх; плашка «nearly double digits · ~2 years»

Think about who that is. The guy who stops for lunch between two jobs. The grandparent who used to take the kids out on a Saturday because it was cheap. That was McDonald's whole business. And they were walking away.
> SEE: рабочий в каске ест в машине; дед с внуками за столиком (сток)

People said it plainly. One customer wrote: "Buy one, get one for a dollar is gone. So am I."
> SEE: скриншот комментария Reddit (ник скрыт), фраза крупно

If you're figuring this stuff out with me, subscribe. Every week we take apart one bill like this one, and the next one might be yours.
> SEE: кнопка подписки небольшая, внизу кадра

## PART 4

So where did the old prices go? They didn't vanish. They moved into your phone.
> SEE: телефон, приложение McDonald's (сток или наш интерфейс без логотипа крупно), вкладка Deals

For a few years the McDonald's app was where the deals lived. Two-for-one, a dollar off, the old prices if you knew where to tap. Many people treated it as a refuge from the price hikes.
> SEE: палец листает экран сделок; ценники «$1 off», «BOGO»

But a deal in the app isn't free. You pay for it with something else. Your name, your orders, what time you come, what you always get. By the end of twenty twenty-three, McDonald's loyalty program had more than a hundred and fifty million active users worldwide.
> SEE: поток данных из телефона: «8:12 AM · Big Mac · Fri»; плашка «150M+ active users · global, 90 days»

And once a company knows exactly what you buy, it can decide exactly what to offer you. In New Zealand, in twenty twenty-three, app users compared their screens and found different prices on the same deals. McDonald's there said the deals were "personalised," and that it's explained in the terms and conditions.
> SEE: заголовок Newshub/NZ Herald; два телефона рядом с разными ценами на один набор; мелкий шрифт «Terms & Conditions» ползёт вниз

Here in the U.S., nobody has proven McDonald's charges people differently. But customers keep posting that the app and the board don't match. And over time, users say, the best app deals quietly dried up.
> SEE: скриншоты постов TikTok/Reddit о разнице табло/приложение (ники скрыты); пометка «user reports»

So now the cheap meal had two locks on it. You needed the app. And you needed the deal to still be there.
> SEE: поднос с едой за двумя замками: «APP» и «IF AVAILABLE»

## PART 5

By the summer of twenty twenty-five, McDonald's had a problem it couldn't ignore. And the fix it came up with is the best trick in this whole story.
> SEE: заголовок Fortune: «McDonald's combo meal price reduction»

In September, McDonald's announced that Extra Value Meals were back. Eight combos, priced to save you fifteen percent compared to buying the sandwich, fries and drink separately. There was even an eight-dollar Big Mac Meal to celebrate.
> SEE: пресс-релиз McDonald's 02.09.2025 «Extra Value Meals… save customers 15 percent»; рекламное табло «$8 Big Mac Meal»

The Wall Street Journal reported that McDonald's would help cover the cost for franchise owners who opted in. Remember that the next time someone tells you the board price is set by the market.
> SEE: заголовок WSJ/AOL «company will help cover the difference»; табло с ценами, которые вдруг опускаются по команде

Headlines called it McDonald's cutting prices. Sounds great. [PAUSE]
> SEE: несколько заголовков «McDonald's cuts prices» складываются стопкой

But fifteen percent off of what? Off the separate prices. The same prices that went up about forty percent after Extra Value Meals disappeared in twenty nineteen.
> SEE: ценник «−15%» прилипает к ценнику, на котором ещё видно «+40%»

Let's do the math with their own averages. Take an average price in twenty nineteen and call it a dollar. Add forty percent. A dollar forty. Now take off the fifteen percent "savings." You're at about a dollar nineteen. [PAUSE]
> SEE: наша графика-калькулятор: $1.00 → $1.40 → $1.19; каждая цифра держится, итог жёлтым

So even with the new discount, the combo works out to roughly nineteen percent above what the separate items cost in twenty nineteen. Using their own averages. And the eight-dollar Big Mac Meal? That was a limited-time offer. Prices vary by location.
> SEE: плашка «≈ +19% vs 2019 separate-item prices · illustration using McDonald's averages»; мелкий шрифт «limited time · prices vary» крупно

That's the move. Raise the board. Remove the discount. Then sell the discount back, measured from the new price, and call it value.
> SEE: три шага на табло: «RAISE» → «REMOVE» → «RESELL»

## PART 6

And it still isn't working. This month McDonald's reported that U.S. sales at existing restaurants slipped, the first drop since early twenty twenty-five. Fast food visits across the country fell more than two percent in the third quarter.
> SEE: заголовок AP от 04.11.2026 «McDonald's US sales fall…»; плашка «−0.2% · −2.6% industry traffic»

In the same report, McDonald's worldwide net income went up nine percent. Two point four nine billion dollars. In one quarter. [PAUSE]
> SEE: крупно «+9% · $2.49 BILLION» на фоне пустого зала

So now they're trying again. A "three dollars and under" menu launched this spring. Their own finance chief admitted it didn't catch on. Now they're testing new bundles in about nineteen hundred restaurants. A McChicken and small fries for three fifty. A national version is planned for early twenty twenty-seven.
> SEE: заголовок Restaurant Dive; табло «McChicken + small fries $3.50» (наша реконструкция, пометка «test example»); дата «early 2027»

Every year or so, a new name for the same idea. A cheaper meal. Which is exactly what the Extra Value Meal was, before they took it off the board.
> SEE: таблички-названия сменяют друг друга: «$5 Meal Deal», «McValue», «Extra Value Meals», «$3 & Under», «?»

So here's how you use this. The board is the opening bid. Before you order, check three numbers. The combo on the board. The three items added up. And the deal in the app. Take the lowest. If there's a value meal, ask for it by name. And if the McDonald's on your exit feels like a rest stop in Connecticut, the next one down the road might not be.
> SEE: наш чек-лист из трёх строк, галочки по очереди: «Combo», «Items added up», «App deal»; финальный кадр — дорога, вторая вывеска

Seventeen fifty-nine was never the normal price. But nine twenty-nine wasn't a gift either. They saw you coming. Now you see them coming.
