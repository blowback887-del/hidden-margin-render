A gas station. Now open their annual report, and the "gas is the bait" idea stops being a theory. Last fiscal year, Casey's sold three and a half billion gallons of fuel and made about forty-three cents a gallon. On prepared food and drinks, it kept almost fifty-nine cents of every dollar, before running the store. Let's make that real. Ten dollars of gas at the early-November average is about two and a half gallons. Casey's keeps about a dollar of that. Ten dollars of pizza? About six. Same customer. Same ten bucks. Almost six times the money. Over the last three years, food and store items were about thirty-six percent of Casey's revenue... but sixty-three percent of its gross profit. Last fiscal year Casey's made seven hundred and fourteen million dollars. And in April it joined the S and P five hundred. From one service station in Boone. So is Casey's a gas station that sells pizza... or a pizza chain that happens to sell gas? Remember our guy at the pump, sure he's being robbed? He's half right. Nobody's getting rich off his gas. Not Mike. Mostly not even the station. The money was never in the tank. It's in the twenty steps between the pump and the door. The cheap gas pulls you in. The coffee, the snacks, the pizza pay the bills.
