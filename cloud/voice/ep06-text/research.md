## Scale
F1. 151,975 convenience stores in the US (end of 2025), 122,620 of them sell fuel (8-year high); c-stores sell ~80% of US fuel. Texas leads (16,504).
   - https://www.convenience.org/stay-current/news/2026/january/26/2026-sees-slight-decrease-c-store-count_research
F2. Industry: ~160 million transactions a day; total sales ~$818 billion (2025).
   - https://www.convenience.org/stay-current/press-releases/2025-press-releases/c-store-foodservice-delivered-exceptional-growth-i
F3. 2025 (NACS State of the Industry, press release 15.04.2026): in-store sales record $341.2B; fuel = 65.0% of sales but 38.8% of gross profit (stated); "inside 35% of sales / 61.2% of profit" is DERIVED, not stated — script uses only "store ≈61% of profit"; foodservice 28.5% of in-store sales, 38.9% of in-store gross profit; average basket $7.69.
   - https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/us-gas-station-industry-outlook-2026-peak-demand-record-margins
   - https://pditechnologies.com/blog/latest-fuel-convenience-industry-insights/

## Where a gallon's money goes (NACS, "Who Makes Money Selling Gas?", 04.02.2025, example at $3.11)
F4. Crude ~$1.85; refining ~40 cents; taxes avg ~51 cents total (federal 18.4; 88 CA … 33 AK) → terminal cost $2.76; retailer gross markup ~35 cents (= $3.11). Inside the 35¢: distribution ~6¢, card fees 8.4¢ (2023), store expenses, equipment, theft → retailer net ≈ 13¢ (range 10–15).
F5. NACS consumer survey: 45% of drivers think retailers make at least $2 per gallon profit (year not stated).
F6. 57% of customers who refuel also go inside. Average car burns 500+ gallons a year.
   - https://www.convenience.org/stay-current/conveniencecorner/who-makes-money-selling-gas
F7. Freakonomics: owner ~7¢/gal, ~4,000 gal/day → ~$300/day (secondary; not used in script).
   - https://rosetta.to/u/freakonomics/gas-stations-updated-the-economics-of-everyday-things

## The 9/10 of a cent
F10. Revenue Act of 1932: federal gas tax 1¢/gal when gas < 20¢; fractional pricing from the 1930s; 9/10 standard by the 1970s; today 0.9¢ can be up to ~10% of fuel profit (NACS); Iowa banned 9/10 advertising 1985, repealed 1989.
   - https://www.convenience.org/topics/fuels-and-energy/why-gas-is-priced-using-fractions-of-a-penny
   - https://gizmodo.com.au/?p=867306
F15. Federal gas tax 18.4¢, last raised in 1993 (CRS).
   - https://www.kutv.com/news/local/why-do-gas-stations-charge-910ths-of-a-cent-extra-per-gallon
F16. Card fees 2025: record $21.3B (NACS release); ~$4.6B on fuel taxes (secondary: MMCG citing NACS Magazine) → "about four and a half billion".
   - https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/us-gas-station-industry-outlook-2026-peak-demand-record-margins
F17. EIA forecasts gasoline consumption to decline in 2026 and 2027 (fuel economy gains, slower VMT growth — not EVs).
   - https://pditechnologies.com/blog/latest-fuel-convenience-industry-insights/

## Hero: Casey's (real story)
F11. 1968, Boone, Iowa (pop. ~12,500): Donald Lamberti bought a 3-bay service station on advice of Kurvin C. Fish; named Casey's from Fish's initials "K.C.". Pizza introduced 1984; by end of 1985 in 85 stores.
   - https://encyclopedia.com/books/politics-and-business-magazines/caseys-general-stores-inc-0
   - https://www.cspdailynews.com/company-news/caseys-continues-expand-while-maintaining-humble
F12. ~2,950 stores (10-K: 2,944) in 19 states; 5th-largest pizza chain per Bank of America Research; nearly half the stores in towns ≤5,000; joined S&P 500 April 2026.
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casey%27s
   - https://businesschief.com/news/how-caseys-out-pizzad-big-pizza-from-a-gas-station
F13. Casey's FY2026 (ended 30.04.2026, 10-K): 3.52B gallons; fuel 42.57¢/gal (revenue less COGS excl. D&A); prepared food & dispensed beverage margin 58.6%; net income $714.4M; last 3 years food+merchandise ≈36% of revenue, ≈63% of gross profit.
   - https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000726958/000072695826000046/casy-20260430.htm
   - https://investor.caseys.com/node/17351/pdf
F18. Casey's breakfast pizza (sausage gravy, egg, cheese).
   - https://businesschief.com/news/how-caseys-out-pizzad-big-pizza-from-a-gas-station

## Our own calculations (authorial)
C1. 500 gal/yr × ~13¢ ≈ $65 a year of station profit per driver.
C2. $2/gal × 500 gal = $1,000 (what drivers imagine).
C4. 20-gallon tank × ~13¢ ≈ $2.60.
C5. $10 of gas at $4.17 ≈ 2.4 gal × 42.6¢ ≈ $1.02 vs $10 of prepared food × 58.6% ≈ $5.86 → almost 6× (both gross, before store costs).
C6. $65 ÷ $4.17 ≈ 15.6 gal ≈ one tank.
