# T07 — материалы для загрузки

## Название
The Sign Said $3.99. Your Bank Said $175.

## Описание
You pumped $48 of gas. Your bank froze $175. The station makes maybe 15 cents a gallon — so who's actually making money off your fill-up? We follow it cent by cent: the cash-only price on the sign, card fees, that 9/10 of a cent, the store built around a hot dog, the premium button, "rockets and feathers" pricing, and a California lawsuit over pricing software.

How to keep your money:
• On a debit card, pay inside for an exact amount (or use a credit card) — no $175 hold
• Check the card price before you pull in
• If your owner's manual says regular, buy regular
• When prices are falling, shop around

The California case: these are allegations in a lawsuit; the defendants have not been found liable and a court will decide.
