Economists have a name for this. Rockets and feathers. Prices go up like a rocket and come down like a feather. It's one of the most studied patterns in the business. One study by a Federal Trade Commission economist found that, on average, prices at the pump go up three to four times faster than they come down.

The St. Louis Fed watched it happen again during the Iran conflict this year. Gas shot up fast and came down slowly.

Why? Part of it is us. When prices are rising, everyone's watching and comparing. When they're falling, nobody shops around. Stations can drift down slowly, because you stopped checking.
