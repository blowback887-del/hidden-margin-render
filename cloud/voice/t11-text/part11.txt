So what do you do with all this? Four things.
One. Before you order, check the app against the board. Sometimes the cheapest version of the same meal is in your pocket.
Two. If you can, skip delivery and go pick it up. That alone can be the difference between a ten dollar meal and a seventeen dollar one.
Three. If there are two McDonald's near you, compare them in the app before you drive. Different owners, different prices. Same burger.
Four. Start measuring prices the way we did today. Not in dollars. In minutes of your own work. Ten minutes for a Big Mac is a deal. Fifty-one minutes is a decision. When you see the price that way, nobody can play you with a slice of cheese. [pause]
