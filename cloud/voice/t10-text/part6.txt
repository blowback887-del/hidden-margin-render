So, back to that receipt from Denver. Twenty-four seventy-five, plus three, plus eighty-three cents for the admin fee. Here's how you don't get played.

Read the bottom of the menu before you order, not the bottom of the receipt after. If there's a fee nobody told you about, ask them to take it off. When the screen spins around, hit custom, and tip on the food, not on the fees. And if a place keeps hiding the price, do what four in ten diners already say they'd do. Don't go back.

And the pressure is starting to show. Florida now makes restaurants show these charges before you order. In October, state inspectors cited a Duffy's sports bar over how its three percent fee showed up on the receipt. Since then, the chain has dropped the fee.

Restaurants didn't empty out because Americans stopped loving a night out. A lot of them emptied out because people got tired of being played. The places that stop playing will get us back.
